# Figma review standards

Registered standards make the design review named, repeatable, and mechanically rejectable when a component claims an unknown checklist.

## button-standard-v1

Use for general interactive and presentational components.

- Match the governed documentation, direct-instance handoff, responsive, token-binding, and interaction-state rules in `PROMOTION-CHECKLIST.md`.
- Preserve visible labels, focus treatment, disabled treatment, semantic tokens, stable master identity, and runtime-only evidence boundaries.
- Complete source-parity, adversarial, and design review passes before recording `passed`.

## data-visualization-standard-v1

Use for charts and other data-encoded graphics, in addition to the general promotion checklist.

- Bind ordered categories to `color/chart/data/1` through `color/chart/data/6`; use `color/chart/track` only for unfilled magnitude or neutral chart structure.
- Keep a visible value, label, or ordered legend equivalent to the graphic. Color is supplemental and never the only carrier of meaning.
- Represent every public visual state as a governed variant or connected interaction specimen; temporal behavior remains runtime-only.
- Verify label, legend, and chart containment at 1440, 1024, 768, and 390 pixels.
- Restrict motion to data drawing or reveal, use semantic motion tokens, and preserve the final state when reduced motion is requested.
- Complete source-parity, adversarial, and design review passes before recording `passed`.
